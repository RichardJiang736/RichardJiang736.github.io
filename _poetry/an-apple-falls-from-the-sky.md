An apple falls from the sky  
Passing the blink of my eyes  
Stretching time as will  
How each moment felt so real  

A swift cut with a knife  
An ethereal glow of light  
It shines and shines for me  
But I could not feel a thing  

Knocked out from a fight  
I lost my sense of sight  
So I’m drifting like a kite  
See how high I ride  

Dye your hair seems right  
Though baby you’re fine  
Your hair flow just like wine  
But I’m afraid when you’re mine
