title: "chasing through the mist"
date: 2026-05-19
layout: entry
---

Chasing through the mist in a foggy mind  
Cogs which keep spinning till the dead of night  
Till you discover the light resting beneath  
At which it will always reside in peace  

Roots that spread into an everlasting green  
While barks rot at a deteriorating pace  
Dogs that bark at the sight of fear  
Elves unravel the kindness that is near
