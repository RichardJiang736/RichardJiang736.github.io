I’ve always felt just right  
When I didn’t notice time  
What’s preventing me from jumping  
I’ve separated this from my feeling  

Zooming out to the horizon  
Why has nothing been brightened  
How my life still feels so blue  
Will I ever get my due  

I can’t see past my reasons  
At the mercy of my visions  
In the haunting silence of space  
It’s just another day,  
But I’m still wide awake
