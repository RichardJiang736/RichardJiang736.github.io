Staring out the window pane  
Tracking where the people went  
Though sunlight could warm my heart  
Love is all I need for my art  

A soft gentle smile to you  
The girl says, “how do you do?”  
Holding that darling smile  
But she has turned away from miles  
Miles, miles, and miles  

Night hasn’t always been so cold  
In the dark, my thoughts take hold  
Our love are bonded by tethered strings  
But you reeled me out when you felt a sting
