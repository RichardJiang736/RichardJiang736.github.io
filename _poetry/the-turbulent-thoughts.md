The turbulent thoughts in my head  
All I need is a rest  
Flowing down the river stream  
I went into a bittersweet dream  

Free from the shackles of my life  
All they gave me is a high five  
Treat it as it is  
But nothing is quite like this  

I got my hard-earned freedom  
To build a golden dust kingdom  
Into the darkest night  
Still something is not right  

Trap by the ceilings of my life  
It’s hopeless inside  
Dug through a deeper hole  
Moulded, folded  
To become just like you
